Still in your due diligence period?

If you haven't closed yet, these tests are best done before closing, while there's still time to ask questions and plan.

Sewer scope

We run a camera through the main sewer line to look for cracks, blockages, and root intrusion. Aging pipes, shifting soil, and debris can all cause issues you can't see from inside the home.

Book Call

Radon testing

About 1 in 3 Utah homes have elevated radon levels. Radon is a natural gas you can't see or smell, so testing is the only way to know. The EPA recommends testing at least every two years.

Book Call

Meth testing

In Utah, meth contamination often comes from use or smoking rather than manufacturing, and residue can linger for years. A simple test before closing gives you peace of mind.

Book Call

More services for your home

Helpful now or down the road. Tap a service to learn more.

Mold testing Musty smell or past water damage?

Mold testing checks whether mold levels inside the home are higher than normal. It's worth considering if you notice a musty smell, see signs of past water damage, or someone in the home has allergies or asthma.

Book mold testing
Air quality testing What you're breathing indoors

We test the air inside your home for mold spores, VOCs (chemicals given off by things like paint, flooring, and furniture), formaldehyde, and common allergens. It's especially helpful for anyone with allergies, asthma, or other sensitivities.

Book air quality testing
Foundation elevation survey A baseline to track settling

We take baseline measurements of your home's floor elevations so any settling can be tracked over time. If questions ever come up down the road, you'll have a clear starting point to compare against.

Book a foundation survey
Infrared inspection Hidden moisture and air leaks

A thermal camera shows temperature differences the eye can't see. It helps spot hidden moisture, air leaks, and insulation gaps without opening up walls or ceilings.

Book an infrared inspection
